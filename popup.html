About GitLab MR Dashboard

A personal toolbar dashboard for your GitLab merge requests. Tracks one or more repositories at once: open/approved/failed/needs-review counts, tabs per author, search and filters (draft, stale, label), and a background service that keeps polling and sends desktop notifications (new MRs, approvals, merges, pipeline failures, conflicts — each independently toggleable) even while the popup is closed.

Your GitLab personal access token is encrypted at rest and gated behind a mandatory password (or Touch ID / Windows Hello, once enrolled) — see Settings → Lock for details on exactly how. Everything is stored locally in this browser profile; nothing is sent anywhere except to your own configured GitLab instance.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by GitLab.
